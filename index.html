About

Cleaning up noisy data at scale is my trade. Most recently at Wilen Group, I built and maintained large-scale ETL pipelines in RedPoint, SQL, and Python that standardized tens of millions of records — automating the validation, transformation, and reporting that let targeting, segmentation, and downstream analytics trust their inputs.

That instinct started at FAU, where my academic ML work centered on hostile data: fraud hiding in millions of legitimate Medicare claims, attacks buried in ordinary web traffic, labels you can't fully trust — highly imbalanced datasets where accuracy is a lie and honest evaluation is the hard part.

The thread that ties it together is signal processing as a mindset. As a music producer I spend hours isolating a frequency from a dense mix; as an analyst I do the same thing to data. Different domain, same discipline.
